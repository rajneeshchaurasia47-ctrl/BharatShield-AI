BharatShield AI is an explainable investor-resilience assistant for first-time and Tier-2/3 users. Users paste a WhatsApp/Telegram/social-media message, financial claim or link; the system detects observable scam/misinformation red flags, shows an interpretable risk signal with reasons, and suggests safer next steps. It supports simple English/Hindi output and is privacy-first. It never gives stock tips, buy/sell signals or price predictions.
